# Block 8 — Credit Lab v0

## Purpose and production boundary

Credit Lab is an internal, deterministic methodology regression environment. It compares independently authored expected behavior with current canonical engine behavior. It does not tune thresholds, choose lenders, produce risk scores, estimate PD, select products, change recommendations or interpret one bank decision as universal truth. It establishes the corpus needed before later Index/readiness changes.

Versions:

- Engine/tool: `PRESCORE-CREDIT-LAB-0.1.0-SHADOW`.
- Case schema: `PRESCORE-CREDIT-LAB-CASE-0.1.0`.
- Initial RU pack: `PRESCORE-CREDIT-LAB-RU-GOLDEN-0.1.0`, revision 1.
- Mode: `INTERNAL_SHADOW`.
- Approved source commit: `6e953e14fb38e535dcf07d39bbef51adc7a09d92`.

The normal upload, source-document, conclusion, dashboard, PDF, scoring and Risk Engine paths do not import or execute Credit Lab. No AnalysisResult fields, routes, providers, databases or migrations are added. Approved Blocks 1–7/hotfix are unchanged. Only `package.json` gains an internal command; all other Block 8 files are new.

## Architecture and files

`lib/credit-engine/contracts/credit-lab.ts` defines JSON-serializable cases, expectations, outcomes, runs, coverage, comparisons and calibration review candidates.

`lib/credit-engine/credit-lab/` contains:

- `common.ts`: deterministic fingerprints, ordering and comparison primitives.
- `validator.ts`: case/pack/revision, identity, dates, expectations, partition and fixture constraints.
- `temporal.ts`: preparation of source material at the explicit cutoff.
- `assertions.ts`: indexed finding, rule, fact, summary and invariant assertions.
- `runner.ts`: case isolation, dependency injection, execution, results and pack orchestration.
- `coverage.ts`: per-rule case buckets and run summary; sets avoid repeated case counting.
- `comparison.ts`: compatible-run deltas and golden revision checks.
- `outcomes.ts`: outcome validation and descriptive observation counts.

`tests/fixtures/credit-lab/builders.mjs` constructs synthetic canonical data; `golden-pack-v0.mjs` contains manually authored expectations and scenario material. `fingerprints-v0.json` locks case/material fingerprints. It is not generated by the runner or CLI. `tests/credit-lab-v0.test.mjs` verifies the pack and Lab behavior. `scripts/credit-lab.mjs` provides the internal CLI.

## Cases, governance and review

Each case has a stable caseId and revision, schema version, title, description, country, origin, lifecycle status, review level, partition, privacy-safe subjectGroupId, explicit asOfDate, material kind, expectation, review metadata and changeReason. Large material is separate from metadata.

Origins support SYNTHETIC, DEIDENTIFIED_REAL and PRIVATE_REAL. Repository fixtures are SYNTHETIC only. All initial cases have INTERNAL_METHOD_REVIEW; their expectations follow the user-specified methodology scenarios and internal implementation review. This is not an external expert panel or outcome validation. The roles in metadata describe methodology responsibility, not named or externally authenticated reviewers.

Lifecycle:

| Status | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| DRAFT | Evaluated, including invalid/error reports, but does not gate the golden command. |
| REVIEWED | Evaluated and visible; not automatically gating. |
| GOLDEN | HARD failure fails the strict command. REVIEW mismatch remains visible as REVIEW. |
| RETIRED | Metadata/material retained, excluded from current execution and gate. |

Partitions support GOLDEN_REGRESSION, CALIBRATION, HOLDOUT and OUTCOME_ONLY. Cases sharing a subjectGroupId cannot be placed in CALIBRATION and HOLDOUT simultaneously. HOLDOUT mismatches carry a prohibition on threshold design. No calibration algorithm is implemented.

Fingerprints cover independently authored case metadata and material. Direct facts and their evidence are canonically ordered where order is immaterial. Pack validation rejects changed fingerprints, duplicate case IDs/revisions and subject partition leakage. Explicit revision comparison requires revision increment, changeReason, review metadata and new pack version/revision when labels/material or membership change. These guards expose accidental edits; they are not cryptographic authorization or a replacement for human review. The runner cannot rewrite labels to match output.

## Material and temporal cutoff

RISK_FACTS mode accepts canonical RiskFact arrays and invokes the unchanged Risk Engine. SOURCE_SNAPSHOT mode accepts canonical AnalysisResult plus optional CompanyProfileData, prepares eligible upstream state, runs the actual Risk adapters and then the unchanged Risk Engine.

The initial 30 scenarios comprise 11 direct methodology cases and 19 source-snapshot cases. The additional defect case is direct. Source fixtures exercise BankStatementMetricsV2, actual Financial Analysis v2 normalization/metrics, uploaded BKI adaptation, actual Credit Portfolio construction, canonical Block 6 finding structures, legal profile and integrity data. Fixture builders specify source facts, never duplicate risk thresholds or severity algorithms.

`asOfDate` is the explicit feature cutoff. Future direct facts and evidence cannot enter risk evaluation. Source preparation uses reporting/observation/receipt dates, preserves eligible historical financial snapshots, recomputes their canonical financial analysis, and excludes future BKI, bank periods, legal profiles, portfolio state and reconciliation evidence. Future maturity/schedule dates are not mistaken for future knowledge. For a portfolio with unavailable later observations, v0 conservatively excludes that portfolio rather than inventing a reconstructed historical state.

Stored `riskAssessmentV1`, `riskComparison` and `riskEngineWarnings` are removed from the cloned source snapshot before evaluation. They never provide input findings. Source preparation and execution do not mutate caller-owned cases, material, analysis, profile or facts. No Date.now, randomness, network or database is used by the core. Optional undefined Risk output fields are JSON-normalized only inside Lab artifacts; approved Risk Engine behavior is not changed.

## Expectations and assertions

Expectations are independent methodology labels, not snapshots copied from current engine output. The initial cases retain their specified expectation when a fixture needs correction; for example, the multi-risk accounting fixture balances its liabilities with negative equity before asserting a reliable negative-equity finding.

Supported assertions:

- Required finding code, optional subjectKey, exact scope/severity/impact, allowed severities, minimum confidence, required and forbidden reason codes.
- Forbidden finding codes, plus optional explicit unexpected-finding policy (ALLOW, HARD or REVIEW).
- Rule evaluation status including suppressed evaluations and subject context.
- Canonical fact knowledge, exact value including null/zero/false, numeric absolute/relative tolerance, origin and confidence.
- Global-stop status, highest credit-risk severity and data-review summary.
- Named methodology invariants.

HARD mismatches produce FAIL; REVIEW-only mismatches produce REVIEW; no mismatches produce PASS. Missing facts are never substituted with zero. Numeric tolerance is `max(absoluteTolerance, relativeTolerance * abs(expected))`. Duplicate expectations/invariants and invalid enums/dates/non-finite values are rejected. Ambiguous multiple matching facts/findings do not silently select a row.

Invariants: NO_BANK_POLICY_SCOPE, NO_SCORE_OUTPUT, NO_PD_OUTPUT, NO_PRODUCT_RECOMMENDATION, GLOBAL_STOP_ONLY_BLOCKING, UNKNOWN_NOT_ZERO, NO_FUTURE_FACTS, NO_PERSONAL_BKI_AS_COMPANY_DEBT, GUARANTORSHIP_NOT_FUNDED_DEBT, RELATIONSHIP_OBSERVED_NOT_RISK, MISSING_SOURCE_NOT_CREDIT_RISK, LOW_CONFIDENCE_NOT_PROMOTED.

Stable mismatch categories include missing/unexpected/forbidden findings, rule status, scope, severity, decision impact, confidence, fact status/value/origin, summary/global-stop and invariant violations. Invalid cases carry CASE_INVALID. Temporal guards prevent leakage before evaluation; future facts are not manufactured into present facts. A mismatch is not automatically classified as a prior-engine defect.

Each mismatch creates an OPEN review candidate with case/rule, actual value/threshold where available, expected/actual values and resolution UNKNOWN. Reserved resolutions include ENGINE_DEFECT, RULE_CALIBRATION_CANDIDATE, CONTEXT_REQUIRED, BANK_POLICY_EFFECT, DATA_QUALITY_ISSUE, LABEL_ERROR and EXPECTED_BEHAVIOR. No automatic resolution or threshold optimization occurs.

## Coverage and run comparison

Coverage lists distinct GOLDEN case IDs in TRIGGERED, NOT_TRIGGERED, INSUFFICIENT_DATA and NOT_APPLICABLE buckets for every registered rule. A multi-obligation case can legitimately enter different status buckets, but is counted only once per bucket. Missing trigger/non-trigger coverage produces explicit warnings, not fabricated validation claims.

Runs contain versions, pack fingerprint/revision, source commit, case results, summary, coverage and optional descriptive outcome summary. Summary reports lifecycle/result/mismatch counts and allGoldenPassed. This is golden regression behavior, not model accuracy.

Run comparison requires matching pack version/revision/fingerprint and case/material revisions. It reports added/removed findings, severity/scope changes, rule-status changes, and transitions against golden expectations. Finding and evaluation comparison uses code plus subject context rather than unstable finding IDs. PASS→FAIL is regression; FAIL→PASS is improvement against the approved label only. This does not imply improved real-world prediction.

## Outcomes and future hooks

CreditOutcomeObservation supports multiple lender observations for one case, including APPROVED, PARTIALLY_APPROVED, DECLINED, WITHDRAWN, EXPIRED and UNKNOWN. Funding status is independently ISSUED, NOT_ISSUED or UNKNOWN. Requested/approved amounts and terms remain separate; partial approval is not collapsed into yes/no. Lender, product, dates, rate, collateral requirement, source confidence and raw decision reason are retained as outcome evidence.

Outcomes are validated independently for subject, dates, duplicate IDs and finite amounts. They are passed only to descriptive outcome summary, never to risk adaptation/evaluation. Opposite bank decisions on identical material yield identical RiskAssessment. Raw decline reasons are not normalized into RiskFacts. One bank decision is not golden truth; policy, appetite, product, documentation and context may differ.

CONTEXT_REQUIRED is a review seam for Block 9, not a context override. BANK_POLICY_EFFECT reserves future policy review without bank-specific Risk rules. Dependency injection for adapters, evaluator and registry permits later candidate methodologies/Index tooling to reuse the frozen case pack without replacing production code.

## Privacy and CLI

The synthetic namespace is `9900000000`–`9900009999`; these IDs are local format fixtures and are not claimed to be valid FNS subjects. Names/purposes are synthetic, accounts/contracts are empty, source/bureau identifiers are synthetic. No real borrower documents, bank feedback, contact data or credentials are included.

The fixture guard checks origin, synthetic INN/name constraints, contact patterns and explicit personal/account/contract fields, including nested portfolio values. It is a constrained fixture policy, not a universal PII detector. Future private material must arrive from private storage; no such store is implemented. The CLI only loads the synthetic pack and rejects private fixture export.

Commands:

```sh
npm run credit-lab
npm run credit-lab -- --json /absolute/new/report.json
```

Default execution only prints counts and coverage. It does not write files, update labels, change code or create commits. Explicit JSON output uses exclusive creation, refuses existing targets and refuses repository input paths. Output inside the repository is allowed only under `output/credit-lab/` (directory must already exist). HARD GOLDEN failure returns non-zero; DRAFT/REVIEWED failures remain visible without forcing the golden gate.

## Adding a case safely

1. Allocate caseId and privacy-safe subjectGroupId.
2. Build deterministic material using synthetic inputs or private external storage.
3. Set the explicit feature cutoff.
4. Author expectations independently from methodology/factual invariants.
5. Start DRAFT.
6. Run Credit Lab.
7. Review mismatches and evidence.
8. Mark REVIEWED/GOLDEN only with explicit review metadata.
9. Increment case revision/changeReason where changed, and pack version/revision for golden updates/membership.
10. Review and update the fingerprint manifest as a methodology artifact through normal Git review.

Never copy current engine output into expected labels, use future data, turn a bank decline reason into a feature, commit private documents, or change an expectation merely to make a test green. If a genuine approved-block defect is discovered, record PRIOR_BLOCK_DEFECT_FOUND and stop for explicit authorization. Do not patch prior engines in Block 8.

## Limitations

The synthetic pack is not statistical validation. No production case database, private storage implementation, external expert panel, real bank outcome corpus, automated calibration, ML, PD, AUC/Gini or prediction-accuracy metric exists. There is no Bank Policy Registry, Context Engine, Index/readiness redesign, debt-capacity/product/recommendation calibration or automated outcome interpretation. Coverage measures exercised logic, not calibrated threshold validity. Availability depends on supplied canonical dates; conservative exclusion does not reconstruct unavailable historical source states.

## Final candidate correction: temporal review diagnostics

Temporal preparation retains the original exclusion behavior and accepts a diagnostic collector. Each exclusion records TEMPORAL_LEAKAGE_CANDIDATE, the excluded material path or fact identifier, the explicit cutoff and the exclusion reason. The runner also diagnoses any adapted fact removed by its final cutoff filter. Diagnostics are sorted deterministically and become REVIEW mismatches with stable IDs and normal OPEN human-review candidates, resolution UNKNOWN. They never enter RiskFacts, RiskFindings or production analysis. Independent HARD expectation failures still produce FAIL.

Covered exclusions include direct fact periods/evidence, BKI report/receipt, financial snapshots, legal profile availability, portfolio snapshots, reconciliation evidence and source manifests/bank periods. Future maturity, scheduled payment and contractual dates remain permitted when the information was known at cutoff. The 31 official Golden fixtures and their expectations/fingerprints are unchanged and contain no temporal diagnostics.

CREDIT_LAB_MISMATCH_CODES and CreditLabMismatchCode define a closed stable vocabulary used by mismatches and calibration candidates: MISSING_EXPECTED_FINDING, UNEXPECTED_FINDING, FORBIDDEN_FINDING_TRIGGERED, RULE_STATUS_MISMATCH, SCOPE_MISMATCH, SEVERITY_MISMATCH, DECISION_IMPACT_MISMATCH, CONFIDENCE_MISMATCH, FACT_STATUS_MISMATCH, FACT_VALUE_MISMATCH, FACT_ORIGIN_MISMATCH, GLOBAL_STOP_MISMATCH, SUMMARY_MISMATCH, INVARIANT_VIOLATION, TEMPORAL_LEAKAGE_CANDIDATE, FORBIDDEN_REASON_CODE, REASON_CODE_MISMATCH and CASE_INVALID.
